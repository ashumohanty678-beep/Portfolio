Design a personal portfolio website interface for Ashutosh Mohanty, a 2nd-year B.Tech Computer Science Engineering student specializing in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, with skills in Python, SQL, and C. The primary goal of the website is to attract recruiters and companies for software development internship opportunities.

CONTEXT

PERSONAL BRAND:
Ashutosh is an aspiring software developer with an AI/ML academic focus and a growing technical foundation. The website should communicate potential, technical curiosity, readiness to learn, and a clear direction toward real-world software development.

COLOUR DIRECTION:
Use a dark, focused base with restrained neutral surfaces to communicate technical ability, curiosity, and a developing software identity rather than an established expert persona. Use a vivid electric blue as the single accent colour for links, interactive elements, and important highlights because it reinforces the technical/AI direction without overstating expertise.

TYPOGRAPHY:
Use strong, straightforward sans-serif typography for headings with a clear hierarchy. Body text should use a highly readable sans-serif style with comfortable line spacing. Typography should make the visitor quickly understand that Ashutosh is a student developer with an AI/ML focus.

TONE OF VOICE:
The writing should sound motivated, technically curious, honest about being early in the journey, and focused on learning through real-world software development. Do not present potential as established expertise.

Example:
“I’m building my foundation in Python, SQL, and C while looking for opportunities to apply these skills to real-world software development.”

DENSITY:
Use an airy content structure. Give sections generous spacing, short text blocks, and clear separation between skills, projects, learning, education, and contact. The visitor should be able to understand Ashutosh’s direction quickly without being overwhelmed by information.

SECTIONS, in this order:

1. HERO
- Name: Ashutosh Mohanty
- Positioning: CSE (AI & ML) Student building toward software development.
- Highlight core skills: Python • SQL • C
- Primary CTA: View Projects
- Secondary CTA: Let’s Connect
- The hero must communicate what Ashutosh does within five seconds.
- Do not describe him as an AI Engineer or experienced Software Engineer.

2. ABOUT
- Introduce Ashutosh as a 2nd-year B.Tech CSE student specializing in AI & ML.
- Explain that he is building his technical foundation in Python, SQL, and C.
- Communicate his interest in software development.
- Mention his goal of gaining real-world experience through a software development internship.
- Keep the content focused on context rather than repeating the hero.

3. SKILLS
Group the current technical skills clearly:
- Python
- C
- SQL
Do not add technologies or expertise that have not been established.

4. PROJECTS
- Make Projects the most visually prominent section of the entire page.
- Use projects as evidence of practical application of technical skills.
- Each project should communicate:
  - Project name
  - Short description
  - What was built
  - What was learned
  - Technologies used
  - View Project
  - GitHub
- Do not fabricate projects, achievements, technologies, or results that have not been provided.

5. EDUCATION
- GIET University, Gunupur
- B.Tech — Computer Science Engineering in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
- 2025–2029
- Keep this section focused on establishing academic foundation.

6. ACHIEVEMENTS
- Provide a dedicated place for relevant accomplishments.
- Only display genuine achievements supplied by Ashutosh.
- Do not invent awards, certifications, rankings, competition results, or other accomplishments.

7. CONTACT
- Make it easy for recruiters and companies to contact Ashutosh.
- Include email and LinkedIn.
- Include a clear invitation to reach out regarding internship opportunities.
- Primary contact action should remain reachable at phone width.

REQUIREMENTS

- The hero must let a visitor understand what Ashutosh does within five seconds.
- Projects is the most visually prominent section on the page because it provides evidence of practical ability.
- Body text must meet a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against its background.
- The primary call to action must remain reachable at phone width.
- The content should position Ashutosh as an aspiring software developer with an AI/ML academic focus, not as an experienced engineer.
- The structure should prioritize skills, practical projects, achievements, and continuous learning because the primary audience is recruiters and companies.
- Do not imply expertise beyond the information provided.

AVOID

- Do not present Ashutosh as an experienced senior software engineer.
- Do not claim that he is already a full-time AI/ML engineer.
- Do not claim expertise in technologies that are not established.
- Do not use vague “tech enthusiast” positioning without a clear software-development direction.
- Do not make academic qualifications the entire identity; the portfolio should also demonstrate skills, projects, achievements, and continuous learning.
- Do not hide the fact that he is developing his skill set and seeking internship opportunities.
- Do not make the design so dense that visitors cannot quickly understand his direction.
- Do not use large gradient backgrounds.
- Do not use rows of three identical cards.
- Do not use glass-effect panels.
- Do not use decorative floating shapes.
- Do not use oversized headings used for their own sake.
