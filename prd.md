# Portfolio Website PRD — Terminal Precision Theme

## 1. Overview
A focused personal portfolio website engineered for **Ashutosh Mohanty**, a 2nd-year B.Tech Computer Science Engineering student specializing in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at GIET University. The portfolio presents his technical foundation (Python, SQL, C), academic trajectory, practical capabilities, and contact points in a clean, engineer-first aesthetic.

## 2. Primary Objective
Attract recruiters, technical leads, and engineering teams for software development internship opportunities by demonstrating technical curiosity, sound fundamentals, and authentic readiness to learn and contribute.

## 3. Target Audience
- Technical Recruiters and Talent Acquisition teams
- Engineering Leads and Senior Software Developers
- Academic Collaborators and Peers

## 4. Core Section Scaffold (Strict Sequential Order)
The webpage structure must strictly follow this sequential order:
1. **Hero**: 5-second clarity of identity (Ashutosh Mohanty), student developer positioning (CSE AI & ML), core skills (Python, SQL, C), Primary CTA (View Projects), and Secondary CTA (Let's Connect).
2. **About**: Narrative on academic background, technical foundation, curiosity in software engineering, and search for software development internships.
3. **Skills**: Categorized, non-fabricated inventory of established skills (Python, C, SQL).
4. **Projects**: The most visually prominent section providing evidence of practical application.
5. **Education**: GIET University, Gunupur — B.Tech Computer Science Engineering in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (2025–2029).
6. **Achievements**: Authentic achievements, certifications, or milestones (empty if none supplied; no fabrications).
7. **Contact**: Streamlined contact actions (Email, LinkedIn, Location) with persistent mobile accessibility.

## 5. Non-Functional & Quality Requirements
- **Performance**: Instant load times, zero heavy asset bloat, optimized web fonts.
- **Accessibility**: Minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for all body copy against dark surfaces, full keyboard navigability, semantic HTML headings (`h1` through `h3`).
- **Responsive Layout**: Fluid breakpoints from mobile (320px–767px), tablet (768px–1023px), to desktop (1024px–1120px max-width container).
- **Aesthetic Integrity**: Adherence to the Terminal Precision design system — dark focused palette (`#141317`), steel gray (`#C6D1DE`), electric accents, crisp 1px borders, zero gratuitous glassmorphism or floating glow artifacts.

## 6. Strict Boundary Constraints
- Do NOT fabricate information, projects, or statistics.
- Do NOT present Ashutosh as an experienced senior engineer or full-time AI specialist.
- Content must be sourced strictly from verified inputs (`profile.pdf`). Missing sections must remain empty with explicit documentation.
